Who We Are

An industrial trading

house, built on certainty.

Spectrum Corporation FZE operates out of the United Arab Emirates, trading steel coils and sheets, construction materials and marine equipment into projects across the Gulf.

Our Position

One desk. Three trades. No handoffs.

Most projects touch more than one of our categories — a shipyard needs steel and fenders, a development needs rebar and cladding sheet. Spectrum was built so that intersection has a single accountable trading partner, rather than three separate vendors with three separate timelines.

01

Steel Trading

Coils and sheets remain our deepest category — full traceability from mill to delivery note.

02

Construction Supply

Rebar, structural sections and bulk material scheduled to your build program.

03

Marine Equipment

Fenders, mooring and deck hardware, specified to berth and vessel conditions.

How We Work

From enquiry to delivery note.

01

Specification

Send grade, thickness, tonnage or equipment spec — we confirm availability and mill lead time within 24 hours.

02

Certified Quote

A priced offer with certification standard, Incoterms and delivery window — no revisions after order confirmation.

03

Production & QC

Mill production or stock pull, with quality checks and documentation prepared ahead of shipment.

04

Delivery

Port or site delivery coordinated end to end, tracked against the agreed schedule.

Standards We Work To

Certification isn't an add-on — it's the order.

Every shipment ships with mill test certificates conforming to EN 10204 3.1, cross-referenced against ASTM, EN and JIS grades so procurement teams can validate against their own project spec without back-and-forth.
