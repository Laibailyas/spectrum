Full Product Range

Sizes, standards & grades.

01

Structural Pipes & Tubes

Structural steel pipe (ASTM A500, GB/T 3091) OD 21.3–609.6mm; ERW & seamless pipe (API 5L) ½″–16″ NB; galvanized pipe (IS 1239, BS 1387); square & rectangular tubes (JIS G3466) SHS 12×12–250×250, RHS 40×20–250×100. Grades GR-A, GR-B, Q195–Q460.

02

Bars

Flat bars 12×3–200×10mm (DIN EN 10058); round bars 4–100mm (DIN EN 10060); square bars 12×12–100×100mm (DIN EN 10059); deformed bars 6–36mm, Grade 460B (BS4449). Prime bright bars and non-alloy shafting bars also available.

03

Beams, Gratings & Mesh

Universal Beams/Columns, American Wide Flange, European HE/IPE/IPN sections and Japanese Wide Flange beams. GI gratings 905×6000mm, thickness 25×3–50×6mm; expanded mesh 1219×2438mm, thickness 1.8–10mm (JIS G3351).
